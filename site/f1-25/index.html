F1 25 Setup Sheet

Setups from the 100 fastest laps per track on F1Laps, for the 2025 cars. The three reverse layouts are included. Pick a track and copy the values into the garage menus in the order shown.

Purple rows are settings the fastest drivers agree on. Copy those exactly and adjust the rest to taste.

The values are averages, so some fall between two clicks (for example 26.5 on a wing). Pick either neighbouring click.

Most of the fastest laps are likely Time Trial laps, and Time Trial turns off tyre wear. For a race, adjust three things as needed:

  • Raise tyre pressures if the tyres overheat or wear fast.
  • Lower the on-throttle diff if the rear steps out on corner exit.
  • Add a click or two of rear wing if the car feels nervous.

F1Laps has no wet data for Netherlands (reverse) and Great Britain (reverse). For those, start from the dry setup, add wing at both ends, soften the suspension, and move brake bias slightly rearward.

These numbers came from f1laps.com on 16 October 2026. F1Laps updates its pages as new laps arrive, so tap the source link for the latest numbers.
